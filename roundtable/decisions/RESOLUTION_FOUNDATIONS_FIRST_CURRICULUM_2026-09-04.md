## Decision

Reasoning-core education starts with the exact abilities required to operate
Axon's real body. The substrate already carries exact symbols. Early education
therefore teaches typed-delta motor control, exact transport, addressing and
termination before attempting broad language or reasoning.

Alphabet recitation is one Stage-1 sequence-navigation exercise, not a claim
that the core discovered letters and not a sufficient mastery test. Every
heldout/regression operation is paired with a changed source, and arbitrary
native and Unicode sequences accompany alphabet/digit work. This makes fixed
song memorization insufficient.

The developmental order is:

1. typed-delta motor control, no-op/abstain, exact addresses and EOS;
2. symbol and sequence navigation, including alphabet/digits and arbitrary
   changed-source sequences;
3. word mechanics;
4. short communication;
5. composition;
6. brother proposal/refinement/consolidation circulation;
7. reasoning from evidence-qualified lived experience.

Prior competencies remain in adjustable spiral replay. Curriculum advancement
is decided by a stage-specific evidence gate. It remains independent from the
complete serving/promotion gate.

## Evidence law

Teacher-forced loss and token accuracy may diagnose optimization, but cannot
prove mastery. Advancement requires complete free-running heldout and
regression evidence, exact typed output and termination, declared-baseline
comparison, source-change dependence, complete-field coverage and regression
retention. Soul dependence is required only where the lesson genuinely needs
private continuity, and paired Soul/field counterfactuals must prevent either
surface from becoming a shortcut.

## Capacity law

Stage gates govern demonstrated competency. They are not field, output,
curriculum, architecture, tissue, context, or lifetime-step ceilings. An
execution tranche ends at an exact checkpoint. A failed gate leads to diagnosis
or a renewable tranche, not silent graduation, lineage destruction, or a
permanent limit.

## Historical treatment

Published candidates, manifests and failures remain immutable evidence. The
earlier ABC manifest is preserved, but its repeated authored patterns cross the
actual train/heldout/regression content boundary and therefore it is not used as
the new mastery surface. The broad L0-L4 candidate remains a non-serving
comparison line.
