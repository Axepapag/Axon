## Binding decision

For the continuous reasoning-Core family:

1. Heart remains the sole canonical Shared Field owner, mask/view authority, validator, and writer.
2. Exact public Core transport is the frozen 16D substrate/Unicode transport directly, through the D16 Core Bus event protocol. A Core's internal model width is private architecture and is not a public transport width.
3. A resident Core receives one complete exact permitted `FIELD_SNAPSHOT` when attaching or repairing, then receives exact versioned `FIELD_DELTA` events for subsequent changes rather than replaying unchanged field content every reasoning phase.
4. Each Core may keep a non-authoritative exact local D16 Shared Field mirror. That mirror is separate from learned/recurrent cognition and never becomes canonical truth.
5. Heart owns mirror coherence. A Core whose acknowledged field/view/mask/hash/event identity is stale, gapped, mismatched, or otherwise unproven cannot count as a synchronized successful FIRST/REFINED participant until repaired or treated as offline/failed by existing participant accounting.
6. Repair uses a complete verified contiguous delta chain when available; otherwise Heart supplies a fresh exact snapshot. Exact mirror truth wins over stale latent interpretation.
7. Existing FIRST -> REFINED -> rotating consolidator -> FINAL -> Heart commit semantics remain. Proposal boards remain noncanonical exact-English collection surfaces. Sibling proposal text is carried in exact D16 form rather than repacked to a recipient Core's internal width.
8. Post-commit `CANONICAL_SYNC` is synchronization-only and does not automatically start another FIRST cycle.
9. Existing D64 packed-rail code, codecs, tests, receipts, checkpoints, and historical evidence are preserved. They remain available as legacy/specialist/comparative transport but are no longer mandatory serving infrastructure for the continuous-Core family.
10. The isolated `D:\ContinuousCoreLab` checkpoints and source are not transplanted into Axon. D512 one-recurrent-chamber/no-attention is the first development baseline to reproduce from Axon's canonical substrate after deterministic transport gates; it is not a permanent architectural ceiling.
