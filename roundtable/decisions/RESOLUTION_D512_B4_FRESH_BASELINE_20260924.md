# Resolution — B4 Fresh D512 Continuous-Core Baseline

Date: 2026-09-24
Authority: Jeff, project convener
Status: RATIFIED FOR LOCAL DEVELOPMENT TRAINING

## Decision

With B0–B3 complete, Axon may begin a fresh local D512 development lineage using Axon's real frozen D16 substrate and the governed Trainer organ.

The opening B4 anatomy is deliberately minimal:

`exact registered D16 -> learned Linear(16,512) -> one GRUCell(512,512) resident chamber -> categorical transport output`

There are no attention heads, no Transformer stack, no tokenizer embedding table, no learned pointer/address motor, and no width-specific D512 Heart rail. Public transport categories map mechanically back to the exact frozen registered D16 bank.

The output motor is variable-length from the start. It predicts one of the 351 registered Axon transport categories, feeds the selected category's exact D16 cell back through the same recurrent chamber for the next step, and eventually predicts one additional **private EOS control category**. EOS is never serialized as a fake D16 substrate cell.

## Training authority

All optimizer steps for this lineage must pass through the existing Trainer control plane: complete inventory, explicit mutation grant/plan, real preflight receipt and evidence, isolated candidate generation, governed learning policy, renewable resource tranche, telemetry, and exact checkpointing. The accepted/live base module remains sealed. Ad-hoc optimizer mutation outside the Trainer is not permitted.

Training is local by default. No Kaggle/cloud dependency is required for B4.

## Opening curriculum and claims

The first B4 gate is variable-length exact substrate copy/literacy over complete D16 Shared Field views. Train and heldout text are content-addressed and disjoint. Training covers all 95 native symbols plus representative non-native Unicode that exercises the byte-transport path. The output class surface remains the full registered 351-category transport bank plus private EOS even when the opening curriculum has not yet exercised every byte category.

The first tranche is a mechanism smoke, not a conversational claim. Evidence must report at least:

- heldout cross-entropy;
- teacher-forced transport-content accuracy;
- teacher-forced EOS accuracy;
- free-running exact sequence accuracy;
- free-running termination rate;
- valid Unicode rate;
- strongest content-only constant-output floor;
- checkpoint and Trainer lineage identities;
- peak local CUDA allocation when CUDA is used.

Loss reduction alone is insufficient. Learning content must eventually beat the content-only constant-output floor, and free-running exact sequence accuracy must become nonzero before copy/literacy is considered learned.

## Subsequent gates

After exact variable-length copy/literacy moves convincingly off its baseline, the same fresh lineage may proceed in bounded tranches to delayed recall and later correction/revision under D16 delta continuity. Those tests must distinguish exact current-field interpretation from private retained memory.

Conversation, broad reasoning, repository-scale retrieval, richer Soul behavior, attention/SSM additions, or wider chambers are not implied by opening-copy success. They remain later evidence-driven gates.

## Preserved boundaries

- Heart remains sole canonical owner/writer.
- D16 mirrors remain exact non-authoritative evidence/cache, separate from learned cognition.
- Existing D64 tissue/history remains preserved.
- `D:\ContinuousCoreLab` remains isolated evidence only; no synthetic codebook, source, or checkpoint is transplanted.
- Current durable Soul doctrine remains unchanged by this resolution.
