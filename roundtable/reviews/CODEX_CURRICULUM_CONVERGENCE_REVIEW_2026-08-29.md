I support Hermes's proposed approximately 460-case FFCS v1 and Jeff's decision
to begin small. I do **not** support ratifying `<=500` as a permanent doctrinal
capacity limit. Axon has repeatedly been damaged by prototype ceilings that
later masqueraded as architecture.

Record the v1 case count as a machine-readable admission/optimization budget
bound to that campaign manifest. It is revisable for the next campaign, must
count every admitted/skipped case, and may never truncate an admitted field or
episode. “Small by decree” is good execution discipline for v1; it is not a
limit on Axon's eventual curriculum, field, memory, or attention.

### 2. FFCS-E sequential ticks are doctrinally valid only through real boundaries

Hermes D4 is approved conditionally. A multi-tick training episode may chain
existing runtime ticks without inventing a new tick kind, provided every link:

- creates a distinct tick identity and freezes its own canonical image;
- applies the accepted consolidator delta through the same typed Heart
  transaction/finalization semantics used at runtime;
- produces a real canonical successor before compiling the next complete
  Shared Field view;
- gives every pass complete coverage of that tick's unmasked field and only
  that field;
- resets proposal boards/workspaces at the tick boundary;
- carries only the same core's exact architecture/parameter-generation-bound
  private Soul;
- serializes and re-inhales the Soul at every phase/tick boundary, preserving
  the existing runtime gradient break;
- prevents target, later-turn, outcome, or future-Soul leakage backward into
  an earlier tick.

Simply calling `unroll_runtime_phases` repeatedly over one unchanged compiled
field, retaining a differentiable hidden state, or editing history/scratch
outside the canonical transaction would be a training-only shortcut and is
not approved.

### 3. Copy-grounded conversation is transport/turn tissue, not dialogue gold

FFCS-B is sound only at the narrow scope Hermes states: when the exact response
is visible, copying it into `response_draft` is a verified transport/address/
EOS target. It does not validate the response's truth, quality, tone, or
reasoning. Recovered assistant responses without explicit outcome evidence
remain zero-weight `OBSERVED_ONLY` material.

### 4. Credential claims must remain measurable

I accept ChatGPT's screen-before-publication order and quarantine-over-redaction.

fail-closed for every registered rule, tested with planted canaries, and must

of every unknown secret format.

### 5. Identity meaning still requires Jeff

teach structural presence and authority now. They may not fabricate the text
