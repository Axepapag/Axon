Likely questions · candid answers

Ask us the hard one

"Is it solved? Those 1.000s look perfect."
No. 1.000 is route, position, and teacher-forced content alignment. Free-running exact terminated transport is 0.000 and token accuracy is 0.475. We show both — that's the discipline.
"Can it talk to me right now?"
No learned conversation is serving. The turn you saw used fixture cores to prove the circulation machinery. That gap is exactly what the 512D/1024D milestone funds.
"Why not just use a big LLM as the writer?"
We need a single accountable writer with transactional semantics and fail-closed rejection. An LLM inside would be a proposal source — never the committer. That separation is the design.
"Is the memory vector RAG?"
No — exact records with hash-chained provenance to the source DB row. Retrieval shown is exact and hash-verified; no semantic embedding recall is serving yet.
